01What it is

An assistant that lives on the hardware you already own

Nothing leaves

Nothing leaves your device.

Your messages, your documents and the model itself sit on your device. There is no server to reach, no account to create, and no analytics, ads or crash reporting in the app. On Android it does not hold the INTERNET permission, so it cannot open a connection at all.

Works with no signal

Works where nothing else does.

A plane, a basement, a train through a tunnel, a country where you would rather not be online. The model is already on the device, so it never waits on a network and never fails because of one.

Your device

Runs on your own chip.

Open-weight models in GGUF, sized to what your phone can carry. The app measures your device, tells you how fast each model runs on it, and says so when a bigger one would answer better.

Pay once

Pay once, or not at all.

Free is the whole app, not a trial: unlimited chat, no message quota, no watermark. Pro is $19.99 one time. There is no renewal, and no month where it stops working because you stopped paying.

02How it works

Three steps, and then no internet

  1. Install it

    Get it from your store like any app. There is no sign-up screen, no email, and no onboarding that asks who you are.

  2. It brings its own model

    The Instant model, 533 MB, arrives with the app. On iPhone it is inside the download. On Android, Google Play delivers it as an asset pack, the way it delivers an update.

  3. Turn the internet off and ask

    Switch on airplane mode and keep going. The answer comes at the same speed, because there was never a server in the path.

04Proof

How do I know it is not sending my data?

Every AI app calls itself private. These are the checks that do not need our cooperation, made from outside the app, on the build you installed.

01 · ALL PLATFORMS

The airplane test

Switch on airplane mode and ask it anything. It answers at the same speed, because the model is already on the device. A badge stays in the header for as long as the radios are off.

02 · ANDROID

No internet permission

Open the Play listing, then About this app, then App permissions. "Have full network access" is not there. The release build never declares it, and our own build fails if it ever appears.

aapt2 dump permissions inborn.apk
03 · IPHONE

App Privacy Report

Settings, Privacy & Security, App Privacy Report. iOS lists every domain each app contacted in the last 7 days. Inborn's row stays empty, unless you started a model download yourself.

04 · IN THE APP

The exit meter

The Proof screen reads the operating system's own counters: bytes out, bytes in, open connections. Bytes out stays at 0 B unless you start a model download yourself.

The firewall test, the complete list of when any byte can leave, and what these checks do not prove: the proof page.
