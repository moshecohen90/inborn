Reviewed 6 September 2026 by the content owner, against spec §6.1, §7.1/§7.6, §8.2/§8.5, §10.5, §11.1–§11.3, `docs/legal/ai-act-notes.md`, Play's AI-Generated Content policy and EU AI Act Art. 50. Branch `content-review`. All edits below are committed; `pn typecheck && pn test` pass (146 core + 50 mobile), pseudo.json regenerated (591 keys).

## Findings

| # | Sev | Area | Finding | Action |

| 1 | **High** | AI Act / terms | Exports carried **no AI-generated marking**, but `terms.md` §4 already promises "marked as AI-generated in the file's metadata and header", and `ai-act-notes.md` makes Art. 50(2) marking a **launch requirement** (Inborn ships after 2.8.2026; no grace period). | **Fixed** in `packages/core/src/chat/export.ts`: Markdown gets YAML front-matter (`ai_generated`, `generator`, `model`, `generated_at`) + a visible "Generated with Inborn (on-device AI). | 2 | **High** | Legal claims | `privacy-policy.md` §6 claimed conversations are stored in an encrypted database **unconditionally**, but the web tier stores chats in IndexedDB without the app's encryption (README, `webStorageNotice`, `storage.backup.web` all say so). | 3 | **High** | §6.1 vault copy | `onboarding.model.instantLine` described **Instant with Fast's plain-language line** ("Everyday assistant: writing, questions, translation") — the §6.1 table gives Instant "Quick answers, rewrites, summaries. | 4 | **Med** | AI Act 50(1) / Play (d) | After the dismissible "can be wrong" notice is closed, no persistent "AI" wording remained anywhere in the chat: the per-answer label read `FAST · ON-DEVICE`. `ai-act-notes.md` checklist requires headers to carry "AI" + model name; Play requires marking content as AI-generated. | **Fixed**: `chat.modelLabel` is now `{model} · ON-DEVICE AI` (per-answer label + airplane test). |
