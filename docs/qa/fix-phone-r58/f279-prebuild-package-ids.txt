F279 — the Android QA/dev variant is its own package, from the Expo config.

Both lines below come from `android/app/build.gradle`, which `expo prebuild` generates and `.gitignore`
ignores. Nothing was hand-edited: the only change is `android.package` in `apps/mobile/app.config.ts`.
The generated directory was deleted between the two runs and removed again afterwards.

Play delivers a pack only to an app Play installed, so a sideloaded QA build received none under the store
id either. Nothing is lost by the split; what the QA build loses it had already lost.

--- one consequence to know about ---

With both apps installed, `inborn://` and the .gguf/share doors are registered twice and Android will ask
which app to open. This is the same trade round 55 accepted on iOS, and it is the cost of the two builds
being genuinely separate apps.
