# Voice on a real phone, hands-free driving — run of 11.9.2026 (iPhone 13 Pro, iOS 26.6.1)

Stream `voice-r2` (branch of `main` @ c05eb89). Follow-up of `voice-run-2026-09-07.md`: UI Automation was approved on the phone
at 14:05, so the XCUITest driver (`apps/mobile/ios-tests/VoiceDeviceUITests.swift`) could tap the app, answer the permission
alerts and read the screen. Nobody touched the phone; no setting changed; the sound came from the Mac's speaker
(`say -v Samantha` / `say -v Carmit`, phone on the desk next to it). Release device build, bundled `instant.gguf`,
`Documents/whisper.bin` (ggml-base, pushed over USB in 4.4 s), bundle-time `EXPO_PUBLIC_AUTOVOICE_LIVE=1` so every
voice stage lands in `Documents/dev-run.json` without the phone speaking to itself. Pro came from the in-app StoreKit
harness (`INBORN_SKTEST=buy:inborn.pro` in the runner's `UITargetAppEnvironmentVariables`), because a Release bundle
ignores `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRO`.

## How it was driven (repeatable)

```
# worktree voice-r2, apps/mobile
APP_VARIANT=development npx expo prebuild -p ios --no-install && (cd ios && pod install)
printf 'export NODE_BINARY=…/node\nexport EXPO_PUBLIC_DEV_MODEL_HOST=127.0.0.1\nexport EXPO_PUBLIC_ALLOW_TEST_PURCHASES=1\nexport EXPO_PUBLIC_AUTOVOICE_LIVE=1\n' > ios/.xcode.env.local
cp ios-tests/*.swift ios/InbornUITests/
DEVELOPMENT_TEAM=NGCHN95667 ruby scripts/ios-add-storekit-tests.rb ios/Inborndev.xcodeproj Inborndev
DEVELOPMENT_TEAM=NGCHN95667 ruby scripts/ios-add-storekit-harness.rb ios/Inborndev.xcodeproj Inborndev
cd ios && xcodebuild build-for-testing -workspace Inborndev.xcworkspace -scheme Inborndev -configuration Release \
  -destination "id=<Xcode device id>" -derivedDataPath build/dd -allowProvisioningUpdates DEVELOPMENT_TEAM=NGCHN95667 SWIFT_VERSION=5.0
xcrun devicectl device install app --device <UDID> build/dd/Build/Products/Release-iphoneos/Inborndev.app
xcrun devicectl device copy to --device <UDID> --domain-type appDataContainer --domain-identifier com.inbornapp.mobile \
  --source ../../../.models/ggml-base.bin --destination Documents/whisper.bin
```
Then per scenario: copy the generated `Inborndev_iphoneos*.xctestrun` **next to itself** (paths inside are relative to its
directory), `plutil -replace InbornUITests.EnvironmentVariables.VOICE_STEPS -string "<steps>"`, for Pro also
`InbornUITests.UITargetAppEnvironmentVariables.INBORN_SKTEST = buy:inborn.pro` and `DYLD_FRAMEWORK_PATH =
/System/Developer/Library/Frameworks:/System/Developer/Library/PrivateFrameworks`, and
`xcodebuild test-without-building -xctestrun <file> -destination id=<Xcode id> -only-testing:InbornUITests/VoiceDeviceUITests/testDrive`.
The driver's new steps: `env:K=V`, `say:<s>:<voice>:<text>` (logs `[voice] SAY voice|text`; a watcher on the xcodebuild log
runs `say -v voice "text"` on the Mac, the runner's NSLog lines stream live), `home`, `activate`, `dump`. Steps used:

- A (fresh install, Free): `launch; sleep:5; tap:onboarding-continue; waitexist:start-chatting:20; tap:start-chatting; tap:airplane-skip; tap:sealed-start; tap:lock-start; tap:mic; allow:12; say:9:Samantha:What is the capital of France; waitlabel:composer-input:capital:15; tap:mic; tap:send; waitexist:assistant-message:90; longpress:assistant-message:1; tap:action-read; …`
- A3 (Free gating + read aloud): `launch; tap:mic; shot; dump; tap:mic-use-whisper; shot; open:inborn://; tap:composer-input; type:What is the capital of France; tap:send; waitexist:assistant-message:90; longpress:assistant-message:1; tap:action-read; …`
- C (Pro): `launch; longpress:mic:1.2; tap:mic-whisper; allow:5; say:8:Samantha:…; tap:mic; waitlabel:composer-input:capital:25; … longpress:mic:1.2; tap:mic-conversation; value:voice-phase; say:6:Samantha:…; value:voice-phase; value:voice-live; … tap:voice-end`

Attachments (screenshots + `driver-log`) come out with `xcrun xcresulttool export attachments`; `Documents/dev-run.json`
and `Documents/sktest.json` with `devicectl device copy from`. Screenshots from the Mac side every 5 s:
`pymobiledevice3 developer dvt screenshot`.

## Devices, processes

iPhone 13 Pro only: `com.inbornapp.mobile` (one Release proof build, reinstalled by the runner per scenario) and
`com.inbornapp.mobile.uitests.xctrunner` (runner); both uninstalled at the end (`devicectl device info apps` lists no Inborn bundle); no setting touched; the Mac's output volume was
raised from 25 to 45/80 for the runs and put back to 25. No simulator, emulator, Metro or gradle. One xcodebuild at a time.
