Bramblewick Harbor Annual Report 2025

The Bramblewick lighthouse was repainted cobalt blue in March 2025 by the contractor Varga & Sons.
The harbor master, Ilse Thornquist, reported 1,284 vessel arrivals during the year, up from 1,102 in 2024.
The new fuel pier opened on 14 June 2025 and cost 3.2 million euros.
Dredging of the eastern channel is scheduled for October 2026.
