# Residential Lease Agreement

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 2 April 2026 between **Johanna Weber**, residing at Lindenallee 4, 10115 Berlin ("Landlord"), and **Daniel Okafor**, currently residing at 27 Elm Grove, Leeds ("Tenant"). The Landlord and the Tenant together are called "the Parties".

## 1. The Property

The Landlord lets to the Tenant the unfurnished two-room flat on the third floor (left side) of the building at Schönhauser Allee 88, 10439 Berlin ("the Property"). The Property has a living area of 61 square metres and consists of a living room, one bedroom, a kitchen with fitted units, a bathroom with a shower, a hallway and a small balcony facing the courtyard. A cellar compartment, numbered 3L, belongs to the Property.

The Tenant receives three front-door keys, two flat keys, one mailbox key and one cellar key. Lost keys are replaced at the Tenant's cost.

## 2. Term of the Lease

The lease begins on **1 May 2026** and runs for an indefinite period. Both Parties agree to a minimum term of twelve (12) months, so that neither Party may end the lease with effect before 30 April 2027, except as allowed in Section 9.

## 3. Rent

The monthly base rent (net cold rent) is **EUR 1,120.00**. In addition, the Tenant pays a monthly advance payment for operating costs of **EUR 210.00** and a monthly advance payment for heating and hot water of **EUR 120.00**. The total monthly payment is therefore **EUR 1,450.00**.

The rent is due in advance, no later than the **third working day of each month**, and is paid by bank transfer to the Landlord's account named in the appendix. If the rent is paid more than ten days late, the Landlord may charge a reminder fee of EUR 5.00 per reminder.

The rent is a graduated rent: on 1 May 2027 and on 1 May 2028 the base rent rises by EUR 30.00 each time. No other increase of the base rent is allowed during the first three years.

## 4. Operating Costs and Heating

The operating costs cover property tax, water and sewage, waste collection, street cleaning, the caretaker, cleaning of the stairwell, garden maintenance, building insurance and the lighting of shared areas. The Landlord settles the operating costs once a year and sends the statement no later than twelve months after the end of the billing period. Any balance is payable within thirty days of receipt of the statement.

Heating and hot water are billed according to consumption, measured by the meters in the flat. Electricity for the flat is not included; the Tenant contracts it directly with a supplier of their choice.

## 5. Security Deposit

The Tenant pays a security deposit of **EUR 2,900.00**. The Tenant may pay the deposit in three equal monthly instalments; the first instalment is due at the start of the lease. The Landlord keeps the deposit separate from their own assets in a savings account, where it earns the usual interest for the Tenant.

## 6. Use of the Property

The Property may be used for residential purposes only. Working from home is allowed as long as no customers visit the Property and no signs are put up. Subletting all or part of the Property requires the Landlord's prior written consent, which the Landlord may not refuse without good reason.

Small pets such as fish, hamsters or birds in cages may be kept without consent. Keeping a dog or a cat requires the Landlord's written consent, which may be withdrawn if the animal disturbs other residents.

Quiet hours in the building are from 22:00 to 07:00 on working days and all day on Sundays and public holidays. Barbecues on the balcony with charcoal are not allowed; electric grills are permitted.

## 7. Repairs and Maintenance

The Landlord keeps the Property in good condition. The Tenant reports any damage or defect without delay, in writing or by e-mail. Small repairs to installations the Tenant uses directly (taps, shower heads, light switches, door handles, window locks) are paid by the Tenant up to EUR 100.00 per repair and up to EUR 300.00 per calendar year in total.

The Tenant does the cosmetic repairs (painting walls and ceilings, radiators and the inside of doors) when they are needed. Holes drilled by the Tenant must be closed professionally when the Tenant moves out.

## 8. Alterations

The Tenant may not make structural changes to the Property without the Landlord's written consent. This includes removing walls, changing the floor coverings, and installing a satellite dish. The Tenant may install a washing machine in the bathroom, provided a water stop valve is used.

## 9. Ending the Lease

After the minimum term, the Tenant may end the lease with **three months' written notice** to the end of a calendar month. The notice must reach the Landlord no later than the third working day of the first month of the notice period. Notice by e-mail or text message is not valid; it must be a signed letter.

The Landlord may end the lease only for the reasons allowed by law and with the statutory notice periods. Either Party may end the lease without notice for a serious reason, in particular if the Tenant is more than two months' rent in arrears.

When the lease ends, the Tenant returns the Property clean and with all keys. The Parties inspect the Property together and sign a handover report. The Landlord returns the deposit with interest within **thirty days after the handover**, minus any amounts the Tenant still owes. The Landlord may keep back a reasonable part of the deposit until the last operating-cost statement is settled.

## 10. Final Provisions

Changes to this Agreement must be made in writing. If one provision is invalid, the remaining provisions stay in force. The appendix with the bank details, the handover report on moving in and the house rules is part of this Agreement.

Berlin, 2 April 2026

Johanna Weber (Landlord) — Daniel Okafor (Tenant)
