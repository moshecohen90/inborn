# Product Team Meeting — Notes

**Date:** Tuesday, 22 September 2026, 10:00–11:15
**Place:** Room "Harbour", 2nd floor, and video call
**Present:** Marta Lindqvist (product lead, chair), Tomás Herrera (engineering), Priya Raman (marketing), Kenji Watanabe (design), Olu Adeyemi (support)
**Absent:** Sophie Laurent (finance) — sent her numbers by e-mail
**Notes taken by:** Kenji Watanabe

## 1. Status of the new app version (4.0)

Tomás reported that 41 of the 48 planned tasks are finished. The remaining seven are mostly about offline sync. The beta has 1,260 testers; the crash-free rate in the last week was 99.2 percent. Two serious bugs are still open: photos sometimes upload twice, and the dark mode on older Android phones shows white text on a white background in the settings screen. Tomás expects both to be fixed by 2 October.

## 2. Launch date

After some discussion the team agreed to move the launch from 3 November to **18 November 2026**, because the store review for the new subscription screen can take up to two weeks and the team wants a buffer. Marta will tell the partners. A press embargo ends on the launch day at 09:00 Central European Time.

## 3. Pricing page and subscription

Sophie's e-mail proposes keeping the monthly price at 4.99 and lowering the yearly price from 49.99 to 39.99 for the first three months after launch. Priya supported this; Olu warned that existing yearly subscribers may ask for refunds. Decision: the lower yearly price applies only to new subscribers, and support gets a reply template. **Priya Raman** is responsible for the new pricing page and the comparison table, due 30 October.

## 4. Design of the onboarding

Kenji showed three versions of the new onboarding. The team chose version B (three screens, the permission question on the last screen). Users in the test needed on average 41 seconds to finish version B, against 68 seconds for the current onboarding. Kenji will hand over the final files to engineering by 7 October.

## 5. Support

Olu said that the number of support tickets went down from 312 in August to 254 in September. The most common topics are: restoring a purchase (31 percent), syncing between devices (22 percent) and exporting data (12 percent). Olu asked for a help-centre article on restoring purchases before the launch; Priya will write it with Olu.

## 6. Any other business

- The team offsite is planned for 12–13 November in Gothenburg. Marta will book the venue.
- Tomás reminded everyone to finish the security training by the end of October.
