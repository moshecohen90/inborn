## 1. Safety instructions

Connect the machine only to an earthed socket with the voltage stated on the rating plate. Never immerse the machine, the power cord or the plug in water. Do not use the machine if the power cord or the housing is damaged; have it repaired by an authorised service centre. Keep the machine and its cord out of the reach of children under eight years. Children must not play with the machine. Cleaning and user maintenance must not be done by children unless they are older than eight and supervised.

The steam wand, the brew group and the cup warmer become very hot during use. Do not touch them while the machine is working and for at least fifteen minutes afterwards. Never point the steam wand towards people. Always place the drip tray before switching the machine on. Do not move the machine while it is in operation. Unplug the machine before cleaning, before removing parts and when it is not used for a long time.

Use only cold, fresh drinking water. Never fill the tank with milk, sparkling water, hot water or other liquids. Do not use caramelised or flavoured coffee beans in the grinder, as they can block it. The manufacturer is not liable for damage caused by improper use, by repairs carried out by unauthorised persons or by the use of non-original spare parts.

## 4. Installation and first use

Place the machine on a flat, stable, heat-resistant surface at least 10 cm away from walls and away from sinks. Remove all packaging and protective films. Rinse the water tank, the drip tray and the portafilter with warm water.

Test your water hardness with the supplied strip: dip it into tap water for one second, wait one minute and count the red fields. Enter the result in the settings menu (see chapter 8). The machine uses this value to calculate when descaling is due.

Insert the water filter cartridge: soak it in cold water for five minutes, screw it into the bottom of the tank and fill the tank with fresh water up to the MAX mark. Push the tank down until it clicks. Fill the bean hopper. Switch the machine on with the power switch at the back and then with the power button on the panel. The machine heats up in about 40 seconds and rinses itself. Before the first coffee, run two cups of hot water through the portafilter and discard them.

## 5. Making coffee

Choose the grind size with the dial on the right of the bean hopper: lower numbers grind finer. Change the grind size only while the grinder is running. For espresso, start with setting 6. If the coffee runs too fast and tastes sour, grind finer; if it runs too slowly and tastes bitter, grind coarser. Change by one step at a time and make three coffees before judging the result.

Insert the portafilter in the grinder holder and press it lightly to start grinding. Tamp the coffee evenly with about 15 kg of pressure. Lock the portafilter into the brew group from the left to the right. Place one or two cups under the spouts and press the single or double espresso button. The machine stops automatically when the programmed amount is reached. To stop earlier, press the same button again.

You can change the programmed amount: hold the espresso button for three seconds until it flashes, release it, and press it again when the cup contains the amount you want. The new amount is saved.

## 6. Milk frothing

Use cold, fresh milk straight from the refrigerator; whole milk gives the creamiest foam, plant drinks labelled "barista" also work well. Fill a stainless-steel jug up to one third. Press the steam button; the light flashes while the machine heats up and stays on when steam is ready (about 20 seconds). Open the steam knob briefly over the drip tray to release condensed water. Hold the nozzle just below the surface of the milk and open the steam knob fully. When the volume has increased by about half, lower the nozzle deeper to heat the milk. Stop at about 65 °C, when the jug becomes too hot to hold. Close the knob before taking the nozzle out of the milk. Wipe the steam wand immediately with a damp cloth and open the knob for two seconds to clean the inside.

## 7. Recipes

### Espresso

Espresso is a short, strong coffee with a thick crema. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Ristretto

Ristretto is a very short, concentrated espresso. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 20 ml. The extraction should take 18–22 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Lungo

Lungo is a longer espresso, milder in taste. Use 16 g of ground coffee and extract 90 ml. The extraction should take 35–40 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Americano

Americano is an espresso diluted with hot water. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml plus 120 ml hot water. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Cappuccino

Cappuccino is equal parts espresso, milk and foam. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml plus 120 ml frothed milk. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Latte macchiato

Latte macchiato is layered milk with espresso poured on top. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml plus 200 ml milk. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Flat white

Flat white is a double espresso with thin, velvety milk. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 40 ml plus 110 ml micro-foam. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Cortado

Cortado is espresso cut with an equal amount of milk. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml plus 36 ml warm milk. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Iced coffee

Iced coffee is a cold drink for summer. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml over ice plus 100 ml cold milk. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.

### Affogato

Affogato is a dessert. Use 18 g of ground coffee and extract 36 ml over one scoop of vanilla ice cream. The extraction should take 25–30 seconds. Pre-heat the cup on the cup warmer for best results. If the taste is not balanced, adjust the grind size by one step and try again. Serve immediately.


## 8. Settings menu

Press the menu button to open the settings. Use the single and double espresso buttons to move through the menu and the hot-water button to confirm. The menu contains: language; water hardness (levels 1 to 4); brew temperature (low 90 °C, medium 92 °C, high 94 °C); automatic switch-off time; descaling programme; cleaning programme; filter on or off; counter of coffees made; factory settings. To leave the menu, press the menu button again or wait thirty seconds.

When you use the water filter, set "filter on". The machine then reminds you to change the cartridge after 50 litres or two months, whichever comes first.

## 9. Cleaning

Daily: empty the grounds container and the drip tray, rinse them and dry them. Wipe the steam wand after every use. Rinse the portafilter and the baskets under warm water. Refill the water tank with fresh water every day.

Weekly: remove the brew group from behind the side door, rinse it under lukewarm water without detergent and let it dry before putting it back. Clean the water tank with a soft brush. Wipe the bean hopper with a dry cloth.

Monthly: run the cleaning programme with one cleaning tablet (see the settings menu). The programme takes about 12 minutes. Do not put any part of the machine in the dishwasher except the drip tray grid.

## 10. Descaling

Limescale builds up in every espresso machine and can damage the heater. **Descale the machine every 2 months**, or earlier when the descale symbol appears on the display. Use only Brewmaster descaling liquid; vinegar can damage the seals and voids the warranty.

Remove the water filter cartridge. Pour 100 ml of descaling liquid into the empty tank and fill up to the 1-litre mark with water. Place a container of at least 1.5 litres under the steam wand and the spouts. Open the settings menu, choose "descaling programme" and confirm. The programme runs for about 25 minutes and stops twice to ask you to empty the container. When it asks, rinse the tank, fill it with fresh water up to MAX and confirm to start the rinse cycle. Put the filter cartridge back afterwards.

## 11. Error codes and troubleshooting

When the machine detects a problem, the display shows a code. Look up the code below and follow the instruction. If an error persists after you followed the instruction, note the code and contact Brewmaster service.

**E01 — Heater temperature too high.** Switch the machine off, unplug it and let it cool for 30 minutes.

**E02 — Heater temperature sensor fault.** Switch off and on again. If the error returns, contact service.

**E03 — Grinder blocked.** Empty the bean hopper, remove foreign objects and clean the grinder with the supplied brush.

**E04 — Brew group not inserted.** Insert the brew group and close the side door until it clicks.

**E05 — Grounds container full.** Empty the grounds container and put it back.

**E06 — Drip tray missing.** Insert the drip tray completely.

**E07 — Water tank empty or not seated correctly.** Refill the water tank and push it down firmly until it clicks into place.

**E08 — Pump runs without water flow.** Check that the filter cartridge is soaked and inserted correctly; run one cup of hot water.

**E09 — Descaling overdue.** Run the descaling programme (chapter 10).

**E10 — Steam pressure too low.** Clean the steam nozzle with the needle; descale the machine.

**E11 — Flow meter fault.** Switch off and on again. If the error returns, contact service.

**E12 — Side door open.** Close the side door.

**E13 — Portafilter not locked.** Lock the portafilter fully to the right.

**E14 — Voltage too low.** Use another socket without an extension cord.

**E15 — Internal communication error.** Unplug the machine for one minute, then switch it on again.

**E16 — Bean hopper empty.** Fill the bean hopper with whole beans.

**E17 — Cleaning programme interrupted.** Restart the cleaning programme from the beginning.

**E18 — Filter cartridge expired.** Replace the water filter cartridge and confirm in the menu.

**E19 — Ambient temperature too low.** Move the machine to a room above 10 °C.

**E20 — Software update needed.** Contact service for an update.

### Other problems

The coffee is not hot enough: pre-heat the cups, select a higher brew temperature in the menu, and descale the machine if the last descaling was more than two months ago.

There is no crema: use fresher beans (roasted within the last four weeks), grind finer and tamp more firmly.

Water leaks under the machine: check that the drip tray is not full and that the water tank is seated correctly. If water keeps leaking, unplug the machine and contact service.

The grinder is noisy: a small stone may be in the beans. Switch off, empty the hopper and check the grinder.

The milk does not foam: the milk is too warm or not fresh, or the nozzle is blocked. Clean the nozzle with the needle supplied.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**Can I use pre-ground coffee?** Yes. Switch the grinder off in the menu and fill the basket with pre-ground espresso coffee using the measuring spoon.

**Why does the machine rinse itself when I switch it on?** The rinse cleans the brew group and warms the spouts. The water goes into the drip tray.

**How long can water stay in the tank?** Change the water every day. Water that stands for several days can taste stale.

**Can I make two coffees at the same time?** Yes, with the double basket and the double espresso button, using two cups under the two spouts.

**Why is my espresso sour?** The water flows too fast. Grind finer by one step or tamp more firmly.

**Why is my espresso bitter?** The water flows too slowly or the temperature is too high. Grind coarser or select a lower brew temperature.

**Can I use the machine at high altitude?** Above 1,500 metres, choose the low brew temperature, because water boils at a lower temperature.

**How do I reset the machine to factory settings?** Open the menu, choose factory settings and confirm twice. All programmed amounts are deleted.

**What is the counter of coffees made for?** It shows how many drinks the machine has made since the last reset, which helps service technicians.

**Does the machine use energy when switched off?** With the power switch at the back off, it uses no energy. In standby it uses less than 0.5 W.

**Can I leave the machine in a cold car or garage?** No. Water left inside can freeze and damage the pump. Store the machine above 10 °C.

**Which beans do you recommend?** Fresh, medium to dark roasted espresso beans. Avoid oily or flavoured beans.


## 14. Warranty and service

Brewmaster gives a **warranty of 2 years (24 months)** from the date of purchase on the BX-500. If you register your machine at brewmaster.example/register within 30 days of purchase, the warranty is extended to 3 years. Keep the receipt as proof of the purchase date.

The warranty covers defects in material and workmanship. It does not cover wear parts (seals, filters, baskets), damage from limescale when the machine was not descaled as described in chapter 10, damage from using unsuitable descaling agents, glass breakage, or damage from improper use, falls or repairs by unauthorised persons. During the warranty, Brewmaster repairs or replaces the machine free of charge. Send the machine in its original packaging, cleaned and descaled, with the receipt and a short description of the fault.

Brewmaster service is available Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00 at +49 30 0000 0000 and at service@brewmaster.example. Please have the model number and the serial number ready; you find them on the rating plate under the drip tray.

## 15. Disposal

At the end of its life, do not dispose of the machine with household waste. Take it to a collection point for electrical and electronic equipment. The packaging is made of recyclable cardboard and polyethylene; separate it according to local rules. Used water filter cartridges can be returned to the dealer.
