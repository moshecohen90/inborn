# Soak run 6 — versionCode 13 on the OnePlus 6T, after a fresh Play install, 22.9.2026

Soak runs 4 and 5 proved the round-14 F33 fix on vc8 and vc9, both delivered as Play updates. This run repeats it on
**vc13**, which adds fixes rounds 17 and 18, and is the first in the series where Play **installed the app from
scratch**: the phone was holding round 18's locally signed release APK, which was uninstalled first.

## Build under test

versionCode 13, versionName 1.0.0; About reads **1.0.0 (13)** and commit **9e1157fab9b8** (= `main` 9e1157f).
Installed by `com.android.vending` at **22:59:16** on 21.9.2026, `firstInstallTime` equal to `lastUpdateTime`, so
nothing survived from any earlier build. All seven asset packs were then delivered by Play: Instant as fast-follow,
and Fast, the document index, speech, vision and both Sharp shards on demand from the vault — **4.8 GB in the vault**.
Build, verification and upload are section P of `docs/qa/purchases-run-2026-09-11.md`.

## Method

`adb -s REDACTED-6T`, keys only (TAB / DPAD / ENTER / `input text`), every `uiautomator dump` serialised behind one lock,
and **one driver process**. Driver in the session scratch under `android-vc13/drv/`, the soak-5 driver with the cadence
tightened for a 60-minute window: F33 every 5 minutes, a `meminfo` / `thermalservice` / `battery` sample every 5, a new
chat every 18, a model switch every 15, and a deep-link visit to vault, settings and proof every 15.

Two captures ran for the whole window: `logcat -v time --pid=<app>` (the app alone) and `logcat -b events -v time`.
`dumpsys dropbox` was read before the run and again after it.

The **F33 sequence**, once per cycle, is the one round 14 names as the repro: HOME → 60 s → launcher relaunch → 10 s →
`am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW -d inborn://vault` with a chat of ≥ 6 messages in the foreground → 3 s → BACK,
which pops `/vault` and re-attaches the chat's whole view tree. Each cycle records the app pid before and after and the
delta in `FATAL EXCEPTION` and in `am_proc_died` for the package. A cycle is CRASH if the pid changed or either delta
is non-zero.

Five further F33 cycles ran immediately before the soak, on the same process, and are recorded in section P.

## The six timeouts are answer length, not a hang

Six of the seventeen turns did not settle inside the driver's wait. **None is a failure**: the app answered every
time, the answer simply kept going past the cap the driver allows.

| time | prompt | seconds | model |
|---|---|---|---|
| 00:48:09 | What is 2 plus 2? | 86 | Instant |
| 00:50:02 | What is 2 plus 2? | 85 | Instant |
| 01:13:53 | What is 2 plus 2? | 83 | Instant |
| 01:15:43 | What is 2 plus 2? | 83 | Instant |
| 01:25:58 | Answer in Hebrew: what is the meaning of the word shalom? | 139 | Instant |
| 01:30:09 | What is 2 plus 2? | 87 | Instant |

Five of the six are the same three-word arithmetic question, and the first time it is asked in a chat it answers in
**8 s** (01:13:17); every repeat afterwards runs long. Moshe watched this on the phone at 01:00 and filed it as **F38 —
"What is 2 plus 2?" runs to the 1024-token cap on repeats**. **This build predates the fix.** Round 19 landed it on
`main` **5338fb3** (`packages/core/src/chat/length.ts`, with `chat/context.ts` and the llama.rn adapter), which sizes
the answer to the question instead of letting it run to the cap. vc13 was built from 9e1157f, three merges earlier, so
nothing in this run tests it. **vc14 retests the scenario through Play: "2 plus 2" four times in a chat that holds a
document.**

The sixth timeout is a different thing and not F38: Instant's cartridge says **"No · Hebrew"**, so a Hebrew prompt
produces a long, rambling answer. Asking Instant for Hebrew is outside what the model is sold as being able to do.

## Honest notes

- Seventeen prompt turns in sixty minutes is low. The loop spends its first minutes on filler turns to get a chat to
  six messages before the first F33 cycle, and the F38 answers that run to the cap eat 80 to 140 s each. The cadence,
  not the app, is what limits the count.
- Whisper decoding real speech is **NOT RUN** and stays a Moshe-only check; section P explains why.
- The run leaves the app force-stopped on the launcher with Play's vc13 and all seven packs installed, because vc14
  will arrive over it as a Play update.
